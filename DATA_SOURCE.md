# Data source

Xu, J. (2010). *Hydrodynamics Studies on Macro- and Micro-Flow Structure with Effects of Particle Properties in a Circulating Fluidized Bed*. Ph.D. dissertation, The University of Western Ontario, London, Ontario, Canada.

The submitting author identifies Appendix B1 as the source of the experimental observations. The dissertation describes the experimental setup, measurement procedures, particle properties and local solids-holdup observations.

The shared table contains 864 processed, time-averaged local solids-holdup observations covering five particle systems and 16 operating conditions. Appendix B1 correspondence has not been independently checked against the full dissertation during this release review.
