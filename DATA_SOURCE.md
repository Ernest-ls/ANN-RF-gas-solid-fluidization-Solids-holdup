# Data source

Xu, J. (2010). *Hydrodynamics Studies on Macro- and Micro-Flow Structure with Effects of Particle Properties in a Circulating Fluidized Bed*. Ph.D. dissertation, The University of Western Ontario, London, Ontario, Canada.

The dataset was compiled from Appendix B1 and contains 864 processed, time-averaged local solids-holdup observations covering five particle systems and 16 operating conditions. Please acknowledge the original experimental research and cite the dissertation and associated publication.
